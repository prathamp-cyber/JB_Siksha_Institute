Medical Program

MBBS Program

Get direct clinical training at government-approved hospitals. Learn in a fully English medium program, structured as per the latest WHO and ECFMG standards.

Program Specifications

MBBS Degree 6 Years

Bachelor of Medicine, Bachelor of Surgery (MBBS)

  • Medium: 100% English medium instruction throughout the 6-year duration.
  • Global Recognition: Internationally recognized by the WHO, ECFMG, and FAIMER, enabling graduates to qualify for licensing examinations in India (FMGE/NEXT), the USA (USMLE), and the UK (PLAB).
  • Dual Clinical Exposure: Hands-on training modules and clinical practice in both India and Uzbekistan.
  • FMGE/NEXT Exam Coaching: In-built coaching from Year 1 led by experienced doctors from Tamil Nadu.
  • Loan & Scholarship Support: 100% education loan support with zero middlemen, plus scholarships up to ₹1,00,000 for eligible students.
  • Embassy Visits: Strong official government ties, including visits from the Indian Embassy.
