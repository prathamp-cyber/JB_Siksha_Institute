University Profile

Andijan State Medical Institute, established in 1955, is one of Uzbekistan's largest medical campuses with over 13,650 students, including 1,381 international students. The institute offers an English-medium MBBS program with strong clinical exposure, and is globally recognized by the WHO, ECFMG and FAIMER. Its curriculum is supported by dedicated FMGE/NEXT exam coaching and dual clinical exposure across India and Uzbekistan.

Programs Offered

The institute offers direct clinical training and standard international modules:

MBBS (General Medicine Specialization)

Offered under the Faculty of Medicine

Indian students are admitted under the Faculty of Medicine's General Medicine specialization — the pathway equivalent to MBBS, recognized for NMC screening test eligibility. The Faculty of Medicine is ASMI's largest, with dedicated clinical training across affiliated hospitals.

Duration: 6 Years • Medium: English Medium
