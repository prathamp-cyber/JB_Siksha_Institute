Medical Program

MD (General Medicine) Program

Get direct clinical training at government-approved hospitals. Learn in a fully English medium program, structured as per the latest NMC rules.

Program Specifications

MD Degree (Equivalent to MBBS) 6 Years

MD (General Medicine)

  • Medium: 100% English medium instruction throughout the 6-year duration.
  • NMC Compliance: Fully structured as per the new National Medical Commission (NMC) rules for Indian students to qualify for licensing exams in India.
  • Clinical Exposure: Hands-on training inside 19 affiliated clinical network hospitals (4,000+ beds total) and a 200-bed teaching hospital directly on campus.
  • Indian Hostel & Food: Fully separate boarding facilities with high-quality Indian meals (vegetarian and non-vegetarian selections).
  • Medical Care: Regular medical assistance, check-ups, and full health support included in registration terms.
