University Profile

Fergana Medical Institute of Public Health is a government-recognised medical university in Uzbekistan, functioning under the Ministry of Health and Education. It offers a 6-year MD (General Medicine) program in English medium, structured as per the latest NMC screening test requirements. Students gain hands-on clinical exposure across a network of 19 affiliated hospitals with a combined capacity of over 4,000 beds, in addition to the institute's own 200-bed teaching hospital located inside the campus.
