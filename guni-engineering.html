U.V. Patel College of Engineering (UVPCE)

Academic Programs & Offerings

Explore top-tier technical degrees certified by AICTE, offered in collaborative association with global industry leaders like IBM, SAS, and Bosch.

Allotted Courses Directory

UG Degree 4 Years

B.Tech - Computer Science & Engineering (CSE)

  • Focus areas: Software Development, Full Stack Web Engineering, and Networking.
  • Industry specializations: Artificial Intelligence, Machine Learning, Cyber Security, and Cloud Computing.
  • Supported by academic collaborations with IBM & SAS for real-world projects.
Careers: Software Engineer, Systems Architect, AI Specialist, Cloud Consultant.
UG Degree 4 Years

B.Tech - Information Technology (IT)

  • Focus areas: Software Development, Database Management, and Mobile Application design.
  • Advanced streams: Cyber Security, Network Administration, and Big Data Analytics.
  • Features real-time projects and rigorous industry-aligned internships.
Careers: IT Analyst, Systems Administrator, Web Developer, Database Architect.
UG Degree 4 Years

B.Tech - Mechanical Engineering (ME)

  • Focus areas: Advanced Thermodynamics, Fluid Mechanics, and Design of Machines.
  • Special tracks: CAD/CAM setups, Mechatronics, and Robotics Lab integrations.
  • Features active practical training in advanced manufacturing design.
Careers: Design Engineer, Automotive Specialist, Production Manager, Robotics Tech.
UG Degree 4 Years

B.Tech - Civil Engineering (CE)

  • Focus areas: Structural Design, Geotechnical, and Environmental Engineering.
  • Advanced modules: GIS Mapping, Concrete Laboratory, and Construction Planning.
  • Features real-world field surveys and structural safety analyses.
Careers: Structural Consultant, Site Supervisor, Town Planner, Quantity Surveyor.
UG Degree 4 Years

B.Tech - Marine Engineering

  • Fully residential program conforming to Directorate General of Shipping standards.
  • Focus: Ship Mechanics, Marine Propulsion, Electrical Systems, and Safety Drills.
  • Practical training inside real Ship Engine Room simulator systems.
Careers: Marine Engineer, Marine Surveyor, Shipping Chief Technical Officer.
UG Degree 4 Years

B.Tech - Biomedical Engineering

  • Focus: Medical Diagnostics Equipment, Bio-sensors, and Rehabilitation Engineering.
  • Streams: Medical Imaging, Biomaterials, and Physiological Modeling.
  • Combines engineering analysis with physical human healthcare practices.
Careers: Biomedical Analyst, Medical Equipment Manager, Clinical Research Lead.
PG Degree 2 Years

M.Tech - Computer Engineering (CSE)

  • Focus areas: Advanced Algorithm Analysis, Deep Learning, and Cloud Systems.
  • Requires a comprehensive thesis and industry research internship projects.
Careers: Senior Software Lead, R&D Engineer, Database Administrator.
Diploma 3 Years

Polytechnic Diploma (CS / Mech / Civil / EE)

  • Focus: Fundamental engineering principles, drafting, and workshops.
  • Emphasizes laboratory operations, machinery assembly, and repairs.
  • Direct vertical entry route to B.Tech program (2nd year lateral entry).
Careers: Junior Engineer, Technical Assistant, Workshop Supervisor.
