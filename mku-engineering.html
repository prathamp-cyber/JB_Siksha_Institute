Academic Programs & Offerings

Explore modern technical degrees tailored for immediate software industry absorption, manufacturing processes, and mechanical engineering architectures.

Featured Programs

UG Degree 4 Years

B.Tech - Computer Science & Engineering (CSE)

  • Focus areas: Software Development, Artificial Intelligence & ML.
  • Advanced streams: Cloud Operations, IoT Systems, and Database Management.
  • Includes dedicated lab integrations and semester capstone project cycles.
Careers: Software Engineer, Systems Architect, AI Engineer.
UG Degree 4 Years

B.Tech - Mechanical Engineering (ME)

  • Focus areas: Thermal Systems, Mechanical Design, and Fluid Mechanics.
  • Advanced streams: Solid Modeling, CAD/CAM, Robotics, and Automobile layouts.
  • Emphasizes heavy machinery workshop drills.
Careers: Design Engineer, Automotive Specialist, Production Manager.
UG Degree 4 Years

B.Tech - Civil Engineering (CE)

  • Focus areas: Structural Design, Geotechnical, and Environmental Engineering.
  • Advanced streams: Structural Analysis, Soil Mechanics, GIS, and Survey drills.
  • Includes real-world site surveys and concrete laboratory studies.
Careers: Structural Engineer, Site Supervisor, Town Planner.
UG Degree 4 Years

B.Tech - Electrical Engineering (EE)

  • Focus areas: Power Transmission, Generator Designs, and Circuit Analysis.
  • Advanced streams: Control Networks, Analog Electronics, and Renewable Energy.
  • Includes high-voltage lab practices.
Careers: Electrical Engineer, Grid Manager, Systems Controller.
UG Degree 3 Years

Bachelor of Computer Applications (BCA)

  • Focus areas: C++/Java Programming, Data Structures, Web Development.
  • Advanced streams: Frontend JS Frameworks, System Administration, SQL.
  • Tailored for application developers and web design professionals.
Careers: Frontend Developer, Web Designer, App Developer.
UG Degree 3 Years

B.Sc in Information Technology (B.Sc-IT)

  • Focus areas: Network Management, IT Security, System Administration.
  • Advanced streams: Operating Systems, Cyber Defense, Cloud Deployments.
  • Focuses on server architecture and data flow management.
Careers: IT Consultant, Network Analyst, Cloud Support.
PG Degree 2 Years

Master of Computer Applications (MCA)

  • Focus areas: Advanced Software Engineering, Mobile Architectures, Big Data.
  • Advanced streams: Machine Learning, API Systems, Enterprise Networks.
  • UGC conforming postgraduate computing curricula.
Careers: Project Lead, Software Architect, Database Specialist.
PG Degree 2 Years

M.Sc in Information Technology (M.Sc-IT)

  • Focus areas: Systems Architecture, Security Audits, Information Theory.
  • Advanced streams: Data Analytics, Digital Forensics, Enterprise Storage.
Careers: Data Analyst, Security Auditor, Systems Administrator.
Diploma 3 Years

Polytechnic Diploma (CS / Mech / Civil / Electrical)

  • Focus areas: Practical workshop training, foundation mathematics.
  • Engineering drawing drills, machine shop practices, circuit assembling.
  • Approved standard state private polytechnic diploma.
Careers: Junior Engineer, Technical Assistant, Plant Operator.
Diploma 1 Year

Post Graduate Diploma in Computer Applications (PGDCA)

  • Focus areas: Office Automation, Computer Architecture, HTML.
  • Tally ERP accounting setups, database management systems.
  • Designed as a fast-track computing course for non-tech graduates.
Careers: Systems Operator, Accounts Assistant, IT Specialist.
