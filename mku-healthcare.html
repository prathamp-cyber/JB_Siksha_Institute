Allied & Healthcare Science

Academic Programs & Offerings

Shaping dedicated clinical professionals, skilled nurses, and laboratory diagnosticians through simulation tools and extensive hospital internships.

Featured Programs

UG Degree 4.5 Years

Bachelor of Physiotherapy (BPT)

  • Focus areas: Human Anatomy, Physiology, Biomechanics.
  • Advanced streams: Orthopedics, Neurology rehabilitation, Electrotherapy.
  • Includes a mandatory 6-month clinical hospital internship.
Careers: Physiotherapist, Sports Therapist, Rehab Specialist.
UG Degree 4 Years

B.Sc in Nursing (Professional)

  • Focus areas: Medical Surgical Nursing, Anatomy, Pediatrics, Community Health.
  • Advanced streams: Obstetrics, Midwifery, Psychiatry, and Nursing Research.
  • Includes intensive clinical rotations and ward duties.
Careers: Registered Nurse, Ward In-charge, Clinical Instructor.
UG Degree 3 Years

B.Sc in Medical Laboratory Technology (BMLT)

  • Focus areas: Clinical Biochemistry, Microbiology, Pathology.
  • Advanced streams: Hematology, Blood Banking, Immunology, Histopathology.
  • Focuses on diagnostic instrumentation operations.
Careers: Lab Technologist, Lab Manager, Research Assistant.
PG Degree 2 Years

M.Sc in Medical Laboratory Technology (MMLT)

  • Focus areas: Molecular Biology, Lab Quality Systems, Advanced Diagnostics.
  • Designed for supervisory and diagnostics auditing positions.
Careers: Diagnostics Head, Lab Auditor, Scientific Officer.
Diploma 3 Years

General Nursing & Midwifery (GNM)

  • Focus areas: Fundamentals of Nursing, First Aid, Anatomy, Patient Care.
  • Designed as a standard nursing diploma aligned with council criteria.
Careers: General Nurse, Clinic Assistant, Community Nurse.
Diploma 2 Years

Diploma in Medical Laboratory Technology (DMLT)

  • Focus areas: Basic Biochemistry, Specimen Collection, Urine Analysis.
  • Designed as a fast-track diagnostic training setup.
Careers: Lab Technician, Specimen Collector, Clinic Lab Operator.
