Academic Programs & Offerings

Approved by the Bar Council of India (BCI), providing advocacy skills, moot court practice, and legal research methods.

Featured Programs

Law Degree 3 Years

LLB (Bachelor of Laws)

  • Focus areas: Constitutional Law, Jurisprudence, Family Law.
  • Advanced streams: Criminal Law, Contract Law, Public International Law.
  • Prepares students for advocate registration and legal defense work.
Careers: Litigating Advocate, Legal Adviser, Corporate Counsel.
Integrated 5 Years

BA-LLB (Integrated Law Degree)

  • Focus areas: Sociology, Political Science, Economics with Legal courses.
  • Advanced streams: Moot Court trials, Environmental Law, Property Law.
  • Double professional degree path directly after higher secondary schooling.
Careers: Trial Specialist, Public Prosecutor, Corporate Advisor.
PG Degree 1 or 2 Years

LLM (Corporate & Commercial Law)

  • Focus areas: Banking Laws, Corporate Governance, Intellectual Property (IPR).
  • Advanced streams: International Trade Law, Competition Law frameworks.
Careers: Legal Head, Compliance Officer, Patent Consultant.
PG Degree 1 or 2 Years

LLM (Criminal & Constitutional Law)

  • Focus areas: Judicial Systems, Comparative Constitution, Criminal Justice.
  • Advanced research: Human Rights, Criminology and Forensic Science rules.
Careers: Judicial Magistrate, Legal Consultant, Law Professor.
