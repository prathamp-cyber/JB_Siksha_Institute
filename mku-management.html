Featured Programs

UG Degree 3 Years

BBA (Bachelor of Business Administration)

  • Focus areas: Marketing, HR Management, Financial Accounting.
  • Advanced streams: Principles of Management, Consumer Behavior.
  • Prepares students for administrative roles and corporate entries.
Careers: Business Analyst, HR Associate, Sales Manager.
UG Degree 3 Years

B.Com (Bachelor of Commerce - General)

  • Focus areas: Accountancy, Business Economics, Commercial Law.
  • Advanced streams: Auditing, Corporate Tax, and Banking Laws.
  • Focuses on fundamental economics and standard auditing rules.
Careers: Junior Accountant, Auditor, Bank Executive.
UG Degree 3 Years

B.Com (Honors) - Specialized

  • Focus areas: Advanced Cost Accounting, Financial Management.
  • Advanced streams: Portfolio Management, Investment Analysis.
  • Includes practical spreadsheet modules and accounting system software.
Careers: Financial Advisor, Investment Associate, Cost Consultant.
PG Degree 2 Years

MBA (Marketing / Finance / HR)

  • Focus areas: Strategic Management, Operations, Leadership models.
  • Advanced streams: Portfolio Design, Capital Budgeting, HR Auditing.
  • Includes corporate case presentations and summer internship projects.
Careers: Marketing Specialist, HR Manager, Financial Controller.
PG Degree 2 Years

MBA (Business Analytics & data)

  • Focus areas: Predictive Modeling, Data Visualisation, Statistics.
  • Advanced streams: Big Data Decisions, Analytics Platforms, Enterprise Databases.
  • Tailored for management analytics career options.
Careers: Data Manager, Strategy Specialist, Analytics Architect.
PG Degree 2 Years

M.Com (Master of Commerce)

  • Focus areas: Advanced Accounting, Research Methodologies, Macro Economics.
  • Advanced streams: Statistical Analysis, Tax Planning, Audit Controls.
Careers: Financial Analyst, Accounts Head, Academician.
Diploma 1 Year

Post Graduate Diploma in Business Administration (PGDBA)

  • Focus areas: Entrepreneurship Skills, Costing, Corporate Relations.
  • Tailored as a fast-track management specialization for graduates.
Careers: Operations Lead, Admin Officer, Account Executive.
