Privacy Policy

How we collect, use, and protect your personal information.

1. Introduction

JB Siksha Institute ("we," "our," or "us") is committed to protecting the privacy of students, parents, and visitors who interact with our educational consultancy platform. This Privacy Policy outlines how we handle personal data collected through inquiry forms, WhatsApp consultations, and direct communication.

2. Information We Collect

We collect personal details voluntarily provided by you when requesting counseling, downloading admission guides, or filling inquiry forms. This includes:

  • Full Name, Phone Number, and Email Address
  • Academic qualifications, entrance examination scores (JEE, GUJCET, NEET), and course preferences
  • Location, budget criteria, and category status (General, SEBC, EWS, SC/ST, NRI)

3. How We Use Your Information

The information collected is strictly used to deliver personalized academic advisory services, including:

  • Evaluating eligibility for degree programs and university preference listings
  • Contacting candidates via phone, SMS, email, or WhatsApp regarding admission deadlines
  • Assisting with ACPC, JoSAA, NEET, and university quota document verification

4. Information Sharing & Disclosure

We respect your privacy and do not sell, rent, or trade candidate data to third-party marketing agencies. Personal information is shared only with partner universities or authorized admission registrars upon explicit student request for application processing.

5. Data Security

We implement administrative and technological safeguards to prevent unauthorized access, disclosure, or alteration of student records. However, no electronic transmission over the Internet can be guaranteed as 100% secure.

6. Contact & Updates

We reserve the right to modify this policy as regulatory frameworks evolve. For questions regarding your personal data, please contact us at jbsikshainstitute@gmail.com or call +91 87804 59636.
