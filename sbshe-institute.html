Institute Profile

Shaheed Bhagat Singh Health and Education (SBSHE) is a Delhi-based skill development and vocational training institute, inspired by the ideals of freedom fighter Bhagat Singh. Registered as an autonomous trust under the Societies Registration Act 1882 and certified ISO 9001:2015, SBSHE focuses on bridging the gap between education and employment through practical, industry-oriented certificate and diploma programs across health, agriculture, paramedical, vocational, and technical fields, with special emphasis on rural and semi-urban accessibility.

Approvals & Recognitions

ISO 9001:2015 Certified

Certified for Quality Management System covering IT, vocational, yoga & naturopathy, paramedical, veterinary, safety, teaching, agriculture, apparel, and sports courses.

NITI Aayog NGO-Darpan

Registered NGO with a unique ID on the Government of India's NGO Darpan portal.

MSME Registered

Registered under the Ministry of Micro, Small & Medium Enterprises, Government of India.

School Offered

SCHOOL OF AGRICULTURE

Agriculture is the science and practice of cultivating crops, raising livestock, and producing food and raw materials for human use. SBSHE's Agriculture programs combine traditional farming knowledge with modern practices like organic farming and agricultural extension services, preparing students for diverse roles in India's growing agri-sector.

Career Opportunities:

Farmer/Agribusiness Owner, Agricultural Scientist, Agronomist, Horticulturist, Dairy & Poultry Farm Manager, Agricultural Engineer, Food Processing & Quality Control Expert, Agricultural Extension Officer

Scholarship Programs

  • Merit-Based Scholarship (based on academic performance, minimum % as decided by the institute)
  • Economic (EWS) Category (income certificate required)
  • Girl Student Scholarship
  • SC/ST/OBC Reserved Category Scholarship (caste certificate required)
  • Minority & Divyang Scholarship (disability certificate required)

Note: Only one scholarship can be availed at a time; minimum 75% attendance is required to remain eligible.

Refund & Correction Policies

Refund Policy

  • Before course starts: Refund possible after deducting registration/processing charges.
  • After course starts: No refund under any circumstances.
  • If institute cancels course: Full (100%) refund of fees paid.

Administrative Charges

  • Minor corrections: ₹200 (e.g. spelling errors)
  • Major corrections: ₹500 (Name/DOB/Course changes)
  • Re-issue of certificate: ₹500

Placement & Internship Support

SBSHE provides structured placement assistance to bridge the transition from vocational training to employment:

  • 100% placement assistance based on academic and training performance.
  • Corporate Tie-ups with clinics, laboratories, hospitals, and agro-companies.
  • Career Readiness Programs covering interview preparation, resume building, and practical internship placement support.
