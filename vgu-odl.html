CDOE VGU - Jaipur

Open & Distance Learning (ODL) Programs

Pursue UGC-DEB entitled Open & Distance Learning (ODL) degrees with printed Self-Learning Materials (SLM), weekend contact sessions, and offline examinations at regional centers across Rajasthan.

Represented ODL Courses

Distance Mode (ODL) 2 Years

Master of Business Administration (MBA)

  • Focus areas: Strategic Management, Financial Planning, Marketing.
  • Specialized subjects: Human Resource Management, Operations.
  • Offline exam options in Rajasthan centers & printed SLM.
Careers: Project Lead, Operations Manager, Business Consultant.
Distance Mode (ODL) 2 Years

Master of Computer Applications (MCA)

  • Focus areas: Advanced Software Engineering, Cloud Deployments, Database systems.
  • Includes dedicated application development lab structures & practicals.
  • Flexible study materials & support via weekend PCP sessions.
Careers: Software Engineer, Systems Analyst, Tech Consultant.
Distance Mode (ODL) 3 Years

Bachelor of Business Administration (BBA)

  • Focus areas: Principles of Management, Business Ethics, Marketing.
  • Includes solid training in financial accounting & computer applications.
  • Self-learning modules & weekend contact sessions.
Careers: Management Trainee, Operations Associate, Marketing Analyst.
Distance Mode (ODL) 3 Years

Bachelor of Computer Applications (BCA)

  • Focus areas: Programming languages (C++, Java), Web Development.
  • Covers Operating Systems, Data Structures, and Database concepts.
  • Self-learning modules with flexible exams at state centers.
Careers: Frontend Developer, App Developer, Systems Associate.
Distance Mode (ODL) 3 Years

Bachelor of Arts (B.A.)

  • Focus areas: History, Political Science, Sociology, English literature.
  • Prepares students for administrative competitive exams (Civil Services).
  • Flexible study materials & support via weekend contact sessions.
Careers: Content Writer, Public Relations, Administrative Assistant.
Distance Mode (ODL) 3 Years

Bachelor of Science (B.Sc.)

  • Focus areas: Physics, Chemistry, Mathematics foundations.
  • Develops core scientific reasoning and experimental knowledge.
  • Weekend counseling support & study guides.
Careers: Research Assistant, Lab Technician, Quality Analyst.
Distance Mode (ODL) 3 Years

Bachelor of Commerce (B.Com)

  • Focus areas: Financial Accounting, Auditing, Corporate Law.
  • Covers banking, taxation, and state business structures.
  • Affordable, flexible structure with offline exams in Rajasthan.
Careers: Accountant, Financial Analyst, Tax Associate.
Distance Mode (ODL) 2 Years

Master of Science in Mathematics (M.Sc. Math)

  • Focus areas: Advanced Algebra, Real Analysis, Topology, Differential Equations.
  • Offers offline exam options in major Rajasthan state centers.
  • Weekend Personal Contact Programs (PCP) for academic resolution.
Careers: Statistician, Data Modeler, Teacher, Actuarial Specialist.
Distance Mode (ODL) 2 Years

Master of Arts in Journalism & Mass Comm (MAJMC)

  • Focus areas: Print Journalism, Broadcast Media, PR campaigns, Digital media.
  • In-depth curriculum regarding media ethics, reporting, and writing.
  • Offline examination options & comprehensive print SLM.
Careers: Journalist, Editor, Public Relations Manager.
Distance Mode (ODL) 1 Year

Post Graduate Diploma in Computer Applications (PGDCA)

  • Designed as a fast-track computing course for non-IT graduates.
  • Covers Office Automation, Tally ERP systems, and web programming foundations.
  • UGC conforming distance postgraduate diploma.
Careers: Systems Operator, Database Assistant, IT Coordinator.
