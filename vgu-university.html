University Profile

Established in October 2022, the Centre for Distance and Online Education (CDOE) at Vivekananda Global University (VGU) extends the institution's academic reach through state-of-the-art Open and Distance Learning (ODL) and Online Learning (OL) programs. Guided by a student-centric learning philosophy, CDOE VGU provides flexible, affordable, and highly accessible undergraduate and postgraduate degrees.

With premium digital support systems, self-learning materials (SLM), interactive live webinars, and robust local student counseling services, VGU ensures learners and working professionals can upgrade their qualifications seamlessly while balancing existing responsibilities.
